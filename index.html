Assumptions · forudsætninger

Minimum 5% udbetaling applies to both ejerbolig and andelsbolig. For an ejerbolig, realkredit lends up to 80% of the price and a bank loan (boliglån) covers 80–95%.

Gældsfaktor means total debt divided by household gross income. Banks typically want 3.5–4.0. Above 4, with more than 60% loan-to-value, Finanstilsynet's guidance means banks normally only offer fixed rate or F5+ loans with afdrag.

Rådighedsbeløb means what is left each month after tax, housing, other debt and fixed bills. Every bank, including Nordea, Danske Bank, Jyske Bank and Djurs Bank, sets its own minimum. The defaults here are typical levels, so edit them to match your bank.

Tinglysningsafgift 2026: skøde 1,850 kr + 0.6% of price. Pant 1,825 kr + 1.25% of the loan (lowered from 1.45% on 1 Jan 2026). The same pant rate applies when you pledge an andel.

Ejendomsværdiskat is 0.51% of 80% of the public valuation up to 9.2m kr, and 1.4% above that. Grundskyld is the municipal rate (promille) × 80% of the land value. Andelshavere pay neither directly, because both are in the boligafgift.

Rentefradrag: about 33% on the first 50,000 kr of interest per person, and 25% above that. Andelslån interest is deductible. The association's own debt is not.

Net income is estimated with 2026 rates (AM-bidrag 8%, average kommuneskat 25%, bundskat, beskæftigelsesfradrag, mellem- and topskat), excluding church tax and pension. For accuracy, type your real monthly net pay from your forskudsopgørelse.

Rates are placeholders near October 2026 levels: F3/F5 about 3.3–3.5% and cheapest andelslån from about 3%. Replace them with the offer from your bank and realkredit institute. This is a planning tool, not a loan offer or financial advice.
