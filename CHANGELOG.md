# Changelog

Boligberegner uses version numbers in the form major.minor.patch. The current version is also shown at the bottom of each page.

## 1.0.0 (26 November 2026)
- First version on GitHub: calculator for ejerbolig and andelsbolig, and the Housing Math Reel with an MP4 renderer.
- Terms of use and privacy notice (GDPR, Danish rules) in the README, linked from every page.
- No personal data leaves the device. Inputs are saved only if you tick "Remember my inputs on this device".
- Security: strict Content Security Policy, self-hosted fonts, no third-party requests, validation of saved inputs.
